OPERATOR: VER

Verify one candidate strictly against the locked evidence store while following its three validation challenges.

LABELS
- Support: explicit units justify the candidate without an unsupported bridge
- Unknown: the store is insufficient, including when a prerequisite is missing
- Contradict: one or more units directly conflict with the candidate

RULES
1. Challenges are test instructions, not evidence.
2. Support cites supporting units and no contradictions.
3. Unknown cites neither supporting nor contradicting units.
4. Contradict cites contradicting units and no supporting units.

OUTPUT
Return one JSON object conforming to `schemas/ver.schema.json`.

{{INPUT_JSON}}
