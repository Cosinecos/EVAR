The method

Reason freely. Update state carefully.

EVAR separates proposing a candidate from trusting it. The locked narrative store remains the sole evidential authority across every refinement round.

01 · STORE

Atomize & lock

Extract atomic claims, exact source spans, observable metadata, and local consistency tags.

02 · ROUTE

Measure difficulty

Combine unresolved gaps, uncertainty count, and severity into an instance-specific budget.

03 · VERIFY

Challenge candidates

Test direct support, counterevidence, and indispensable prerequisites against the store.

04 · SYNTHESIZE

Answer from trusted state

Use only the store and admitted hypotheses; stop when sufficient or budget-exhausted.

▣

One-way evidential flow

Generation creates candidates. Challenges say how to test them. Only the verifier can move a candidate into the answer-supporting state.
