EVAR

Evidence-Validated Hypothesis Admission for budget-aware narrative reasoning.

EVAR separates hypothesis generation from hypothesis admission, preventing plausible but unsupported intermediate conclusions from entering the trusted reasoning state too early.

The method

Reason freely. Update state carefully.

EVAR keeps the source narrative as the evidential authority throughout reasoning. Candidate explanations may be generated freely, but only hypotheses that pass explicit evidence validation can enter the trusted state used for final synthesis.

01 · STORE

Build the evidence store

Decompose the narrative into source-linked evidence units while preserving observable entities, temporal relations, polarity, and local consistency information.

02 · ROUTE

Estimate difficulty

Identify unresolved gaps and use instance complexity to allocate a bounded reasoning budget rather than applying the same refinement depth to every case.

03 · VERIFY

Challenge candidates

Check direct support, counterevidence, and indispensable prerequisites before deciding whether a candidate may enter the trusted state.

04 · SYNTHESIZE

Answer from trusted state

Continue while blocking gaps remain and budget permits, then synthesize the final answer from the evidence store and admitted hypotheses only.

▣

One-way evidential flow

Generation creates possibilities, but generation alone cannot modify the trusted reasoning state. A candidate becomes usable for final synthesis only after validation against source evidence.

SUPPORT / UNKNOWN / CONTRADICT Explicit admission decisions

Support admits a hypothesis, Unknown quarantines it, and Contradict removes it from consideration.

BUDGET-AWARE REFINEMENT Stop when evidence is sufficient

Refinement ends when no blocking gap remains, the evidence is sufficient, or the allocated reasoning budget is exhausted.
