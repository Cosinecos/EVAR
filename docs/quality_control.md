# Quality-Control Notes

The released materials support structural checks for:

- required files and annotation fields;
- split sizes and case identifiers;
- presence of the gold culprit in the candidate set;
- consistency between reference answers and annotations;
- evidence-cue and suspect counts;
- difficulty-related ranges and reference mappings.

These checks detect formatting and consistency problems. They do not by themselves establish narrative originality, unique solvability, evidentiary sufficiency, or the absence of ambiguity.

The repository provides manual-review templates for verdict uniqueness, intent support, action support, evidence alignment, distractor quality, and annotation consistency. These files describe a review procedure. Unless completed review records are released, they should not be described as proof that all 300 cases were independently human-audited.

Any future correction should record the affected case identifiers, the changed files, the reason for the change, and whether previously reported results remain comparable.
